1. Strict No-Refund Policy

⚠️ All Purchases Are Final & Non-Refundable

Please read this policy carefully before completing any payment. Budcast maintains a strict NO REFUND policy on all software subscriptions, license upgrades, Organizer Pro plans, Business Enterprise packages, and digital services. Once a transaction is successfully processed via Razorpay, no refunds, partial refunds, or charge credits will be issued under any circumstances.

By purchasing any paid plan or subscription on Budcast, you explicitly acknowledge, agree, and accept that your payment is non-refundable and that you waive any statutory right to a cooling-off period or refund once digital provisioning has occurred.

2. Reason for No-Refund Policy (Instant Digital Delivery)

Budcast provides intangible, irrevocable digital software licenses and real-time synchronization infrastructure:

  • Instant Provisioning: As soon as your payment is authorized via Razorpay, your host device is immediately unlocked with multi-DJ channels (Red, Blue, Green), 150+ earbud listener slots, and lossless 320kbps audio.
  • Irrevocable Digital Service: Because digital software access, bandwidth allocation, and license entitlements are delivered instantaneously and cannot be physically returned or unconsumed, all sales are considered final upon checkout.

3. Subscription Cancellation & Stopping Future Charges

While past payments are non-refundable, you maintain complete control over your subscription renewals:

  • Cancel Anytime: You can cancel your recurring monthly or yearly subscription at any time prior to your next renewal date.
  • How to Cancel: You can cancel self-service within the app settings under Plans & Subscriptions or by emailing support@budcast.live with your registered email address.
  • Access Through Billing Period: When you cancel your subscription, your paid privileges remain active until the end of your current prepaid billing period. At the end of the period, your account automatically reverts to the Free Starter tier with zero future charges.

4. Free Starter Tier & Evaluation

To ensure that Budcast meets your technical expectations and hardware requirements before spending any money:

  • We offer a 100% Free Starter Cinema Tier that allows you to test local Wi-Fi mesh synchronization, sub-millisecond phase-locking, and wireless earbud audio with up to 8 listeners at zero cost.
  • We strongly encourage all organizers, DJs, and event hosts to test our free tier on their venue's Wi-Fi router network prior to upgrading to a paid tier.

5. Accidental Duplicate Transactions

In the rare event of a technical network error where you were accidentally billed multiple times for the exact same order or billing period due to a gateway timeout:

  • Our billing team will promptly verify the duplicate charge with Razorpay.
  • Once verified as an accidental duplicate payment, the duplicate amount will be reversed back to your original payment source within 5–7 business days.
