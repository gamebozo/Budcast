📦 Shipping & Digital Delivery Policy

⚡ Instant Electronic Delivery (0 Seconds)

Budcast is an entirely digital software and SaaS application. No physical products or tangible goods are shipped. All software licenses, subscription activations, and host room upgrades are provisioned electronically and instantaneously upon successful payment confirmation.

1. Delivery Method & Fulfillment

  • In-App Activation: As soon as your payment is authorized via Razorpay, your host device is automatically unlocked with Pro or Business privileges without requiring any software restart or manual key entry.
  • Email Confirmation & Invoice: A digital tax invoice and payment receipt containing your Order ID and subscription period is immediately dispatched to your registered email address.

2. Shipping Fees & Taxes

Because delivery is 100% electronic via internet network provisioning, there are zero shipping fees or courier charges (₹0.00). Applicable statutory goods and services taxes (GST) are clearly itemized during checkout.

3. Support for Delivery Issues

If for any reason your subscription plan does not reflect in your account within 60 seconds of a successful transaction, please contact our instant billing desk at billing@budcast.live with your Razorpay Payment ID for immediate manual license refresh.
