2. Description of Services

Budcast provides a sub-millisecond local wireless audio and video synchronization platform that enables users to broadcast media from a host device directly to connected attendee wireless earbuds and smartphones over local Wi-Fi networks without requiring cellular data consumption or traditional Bluetooth pairing limits.

💡 Local Mesh Architecture

Budcast operates directly across local area network (LAN/WLAN) socket connections. Media streams and audio buffers are transmitted locally between participant devices and are not stored, recorded, or monitored on central cloud servers.

We offer free introductory tiers as well as premium subscription tiers (Organizer Pro, Business Enterprise, and VIP Founder programs) offering multi-DJ channel broadcasting, high-capacity earbud listeners, lossless 320kbps audio, and real-time attendance management.
