1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

At Budcast (operated by Budcast Technologies), we believe that audio entertainment, silent disco events, and cinema watch parties should be shared with friends without sacrificing personal privacy or sending your media streams across the public internet.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and safeguard information when you visit our website, register for pre-launch VIP early access, utilize our synchronized audio streaming applications, or purchase subscription plans through our payment gateway.

2. Zero-Cloud Local Mesh Audio Privacy

🛡️ 100% Local Wi-Fi Broadcasting

No Cloud Media Storage: The movie video files, music tracks, microphone shoutouts, and audio packets broadcast from a Host device to attendee earbuds travel exclusively over your local Wi-Fi router. Budcast does NOT upload, record, eavesdrop, or store your private audio or video media on remote cloud servers.

Because media streams remain within your local network perimeter, your bandwidth is preserved, cellular data usage is zero, and your listening session remains completely private to the people in your room.

3. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal personal information necessary to deliver, authenticate, and improve our services:

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Pre-Launch & VIP Waitlist: When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address, chosen role (Host, DJ, Cinema, Beta Tester), and optional referral link tracking.
  • Support Inquiries: When you contact our support desk via our contact form or email, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message details.

B. Subscription & Billing Information

When you purchase or renew an Organizer Pro or Business Enterprise plan, billing transactions are handled directly by our certified payment processor, Razorpay. We receive transaction confirmation metadata (Order ID, Payment ID, Plan Name, Billing Period, Amount, Timestamp, and Customer Email). We never view, process, or store raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or banking passwords.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Information

  • Clock Sync & Latency Telemetry: Anonymized millisecond ping time and jitter metrics used by our phase-locking algorithm to calibrate earbud alignment.
  • Device Metadata: Operating system version, browser type, and earbud preset offset preference (e.g., AirPods, Galaxy Buds, Sony WH-1000XM5) stored locally on your device.

4. Payment Security with Razorpay

All online payments on Budcast are powered by Razorpay Software Private Limited, an ISO/IEC 27001 certified and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway.

Razorpay uses end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption to protect your sensitive financial data during checkout. For more information on how Razorpay secures transactions, please consult the Razorpay Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for lawful purposes, including:

  • Provisioning paid subscription features, multi-DJ stages, and listener capacity upgrades.
  • Dispatching VIP early access codes, beta release announcements, and transactional receipts.
  • Responding to support tickets, troubleshooting device synchronization, and resolving technical inquiries.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions, unauthorized key generation, and network security breaches.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

We only share data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform:

  • Payment Gateway: Razorpay (for secure payment processing and subscription billing).
  • Transactional Email: Resend / Brevo (for dispatching VIP pass codes, receipts, and security alerts).
  • Cloud Hosting: Render / AWS (for hosting our static web pages and web socket signaling relay).

7. Cookies & Local Storage

Budcast uses standard browser LocalStorage and minimal session cookies solely to preserve your active VIP queue pass, host license preferences, dark-mode styling, and earbud delay calibration. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising tracking cookies.

8. Your Privacy Rights & Data Erasure

You have the right at any time to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data associated with your email address.
  • Request correction or rectification of any inaccurate information.
  • Request complete erasure and deletion of your account, waitlist reservation, and support tickets.
  • Opt out of marketing or promotional emails by clicking Unsubscribe in any email.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@budcast.live with your registered email address.
